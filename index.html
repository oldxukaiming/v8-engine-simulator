INTERACTIVE V8

A real-time mechanical study of a 90° cross-plane V8. Every piston, connecting rod, valve and camshaft is driven by the same crank-angle model that produces the firing order.

01

ORBIT & INSPECT

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Use the four camera presets for repeatable engineering views.

02

RUN THE ENGINE

Press START or the space bar. Throttle changes engine speed; dyno load trims the achievable RPM. Mechanical time warp only slows the visuals.

03

READ THE CYCLE

Click any firing-order number to focus that cylinder. The 720° strip shows its intake, compression, power and exhaust strokes.

04

CHANGE THE VIEW

Cutaway, X-Ray, Exploded and Combustion modes progressively reveal the internal assembly and combustion events.
